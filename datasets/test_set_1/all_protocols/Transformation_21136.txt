Title: Electroporation of Thalassiosira pseudonana

Description:
An electroporation-mediated genetic transformation or the marine diatom Thalassiosira pseudonana was developed. Using a sorbitol-based buffer, T. pseudonana cells were successfully transformed with the Tpfcp/nat plasmid at an efficiency of 2820 per 108 cells.  This  represents a six-fold improvement compared with previously published methods.
