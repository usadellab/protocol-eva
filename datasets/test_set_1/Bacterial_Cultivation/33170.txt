Title: Marchantia high throughput imaging in multiwell plates

Description:
This protocol allows high throughput imaging of Marchantia gemmae, using a cheap setup made with broadly available lab equipment. It was developed from the 3D-printed Imaging Growth Chamber developed by Mihails Delmans in his PhD work (https://github.com/HaseloffLab/MihailsDelmansThesis).
We used a transparent 384 wells plate filled with 1/2 strength Gamborg B5 media with 1.2% agar and placed a single gemma at the centre of the well. Gene frames and coverslips treated with anti-fog spray were used to cover (seal) the wells. This setup can be paired with automated imaging of samples.
