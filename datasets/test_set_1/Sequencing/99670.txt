Title: Podocoryna ACME cell dissociations - draft v1.0, May 13 2024

Description:
This protocol is intended to dissociate and fix cells of the hydrozoan Podocoryna carnea for single-cell RNA sequencing on the 10X Chromium platform following a FACS sorting step. ACME-fixed Podocoryna cells maintain their morphology for use in microscopy and can also be frozen at -20C for storage and/or shipping.
