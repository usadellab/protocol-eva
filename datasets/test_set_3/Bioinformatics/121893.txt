Title: Barcoding lentiviral Cre vectors for use in experiments involving downstream Tuba-seq analysis.

Description:
This protocol outlines the procedure to massively barcode Lentiviral Cre vectors. This procedure was developed for use of the Tuba-seq pipeline to calculate tumor sizes in lentivirally inducible mouse models of cancer. The protocol accompanies Rogers et al, Nature Methods, published online May 22, 2017 (nmeth.4297).
