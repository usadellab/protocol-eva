Title: Small volume viral RNA extraction using MagMAX  Viral RNA Isolation Kit 

Description:
This protocol describes the viral nucleic acid recovery and purification from stool suspensions. The method utilises the magnetic bead based MagMAX ‱ Viral  RNA Isolation Kit to purify nucleic acid from 300 µL of sample. To perform manual extractions follow Workflow A, for automated extraction on King Fisher Duo Prime follow steps in Workflow B, and for automated extraction on King Fisher Flex follow Workflow C. 
Version 2 has a few minor edits to text for better clarity and updated settings to match specifications across the attached extraction programs for King Fisher Duo Prime and King Fisher Flex devices.
