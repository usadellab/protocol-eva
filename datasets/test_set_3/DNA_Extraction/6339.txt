Title: Isolation of Artemia nauplii for DNA extraction

Description:
Nauplii, ie newly hatched Artmemia (3-5 days), are processed to concentrate and separate from the eggs, before a DNA extraction


=== PROTOCOL STEPS ===

## Step 1: Prepare conical filter from Whatman #1

## Step 2: Place in glass funnel over beaker

## Step 3: Pour in Artemia culture and let drain through

## Step 4: Remove Artemia from centre of paper to 1.5ml tube

## Step 5: Spin for 30 seconds in microfuge to pellet Artemia

## Step 6: Discard supernatant

## Step 7: Spin for 30 seconds in microfuge to pellet Artemia

## Step 8: Discard supernatant
