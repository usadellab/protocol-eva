Title: Functionality test (TBE  electrophoresis buffer)

Description:
This protocol describes the comparative analysis of resolution of bands and band patterns after electrophoresis with the BenBio TBE  buffer formulation as compared with a commercial TBE buffer. We use 0.5 kb and 1kb PCR amplicons loaded on 1.5% (w/v) agarose gel, run electrophoresis at 80V and visualized on a Blue light transilluminator or UV transilluminator.
Our formulation of TBE buffer is able to allow us obtain a higher resolution of smaller DNA fragments on agarose gels.
