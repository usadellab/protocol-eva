Title: Polyclonal Allelic Expression Assay Protocol

Description:
Polyclonal allelic expression assay for detecting the regulatory effects of genetic variants within transcripts using CRISPR/Cas9 genome editing and amplicon sequencing.


=== PROTOCOL STEPS ===

- Doxycycline (Hyclate) 1 g
- Nextera XT Index Kit, 96 indices, 384 samples
- TrypLE™ Express Enzyme
- AmpureXP beads 
- TRIzol&trade; LS Reagent
- SuperScript&trade; IV VILO&trade; Master Mix with ezDNase&trade; Enzyme
