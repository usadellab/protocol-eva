Title: Hepatitis E and Enterovirus Multiplex Nested RT-PCR

Description:

For1_5NCR: CGGTACCYTTGTRCGCCTG
Rev7_5NCR: ATTGTCACCATAAGCAGCC
Inner EV: For2_5NCR and Rev6_5NCR (388 bps)
For2_5NCR: CAAGCACTTCTGTTWCCC
