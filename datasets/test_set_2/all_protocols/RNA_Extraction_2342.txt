Title: RNA extraction using the 'home-made' Trizol substitute

Description:
A simple protocol for RNA extraction from various types of samples (protists, fungi, bacteria, organelles, subcellular fractions, ribosomes, etc.). Compared to the commercial Trizol® procedure, it makes use of generally available chemicals without the need for columns, thus allowing concurrent extraction of transcripts of all sizes (including RNAs <200 nt). Small circular DNA molecules (up to ~9 kbp) will also be efficiently isolated. The procedure is based on a previously published protocol by Rodríguez-Ezpeleta et al. (DOI: 10.1007/978-1-60327-136-3_3).

=== MATERIALS ===
- Isopropanol
- Glycerol

- Sodium Acetate, Trihydrate

- Ethanol
- Glycogen
