Title: Stable transfection protocol of unicellular relative to animals Corallochytrium limacisporum


=== PROTOCOL STEPS ===

## Step 1: Perform transfection following transient transfection protocol

## Step 2: After 3 days electroporation add puromycin as a selective agent

## Step 3: After 7 days electroporation, centrifuge cells and resuspend with fresh medium and puromycin

## Step 4: Keep doing 1:2 dilutions every 3-4 days

## Step 5: Approximately after 20 days electroporation the culture should be mainly composed of transformants

## Step 6: Plate 100 uL of the transformed culture into marine agar plates with puromycin for single colony isolation

## Step 7: Grow single colonies in fresh medium without puromycin
